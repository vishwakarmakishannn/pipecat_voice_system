## What is Mswipe's Wisepos Plus?

- Wisepos Plus is a smart, Android-based point-of-sale (POS) device designed by Mswipe to simplify payment processing for businesses. It integrates seamlessly with business systems, enabling real-time transaction tracking, invoicing, and efficient management. Its intuitive interface and portable design make it an ideal solution for businesses seeking reliable and user-friendly payment devices.

## What payment methods and card types does the Wisepos Plus support?

- Wisepos Plus offers extensive payment options, including:

- UPI Payments: Fully supports UPI for fast and secure digital transactions.

- Contactless Payments: Equipped with NFC technology for seamless contactless payments using compatible cards and devices.

- Card Payments: Accepts a wide range of cards, including Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, and other major credit and debit cards.

## Can Wisepos Plus print receipts?

- Yes, Wisepos Plus is equipped with a built-in printer, allowing you to generate and print receipts for your customers instantly.

## How do I integrate Wisepos Plus with my existing billing system?

- Wisepos Plus can be integrated seamlessly with your existing billing system using Mswipe's API and technical support team. Detailed documentation is provided to guide you through the process.

## What are the connectivity options for Wisepos Plus?

- Wisepos Plus offers multiple connectivity options, including Wi-Fi, 4G, and Bluetooth, ensuring uninterrupted payment processing.

## Is Wisepos Plus compatible with NFC payments?

- Yes, Wisepos Plus is equipped with NFC capabilities, enabling contactless payments through cards and devices.

## Can I manage my transaction history on Wisepos Plus?

- Yes, you can view and manage your transaction history directly on the device on the Mswipe Merchant app or through Mswipe's merchant portal.
