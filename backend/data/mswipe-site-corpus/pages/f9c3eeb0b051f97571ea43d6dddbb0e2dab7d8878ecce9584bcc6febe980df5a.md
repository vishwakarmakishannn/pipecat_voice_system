## What is Mswipe Payment Gateway and how does the Mswipe Payment Gateway work?

- Mswipe Payment Gateway is a secure and scalable solution designed for integration with web and e-commerce businesses. It enables secure payment collection and transaction processing via UPI, cards, or net banking. The gateway securely transfers payment information between the customer, merchant, and payment processor, ensuring real-time authorization and settlement for fast and efficient transactions.

- Yes, the Mswipe Payment Gateway is highly secure. It is certified with PCI DSS compliance and operates under the Payment Aggregator license from the RBI. Mswipe also holds a PPI license and adheres to strict regulatory and compliance standards. All transactions are processed through encrypted and secure channels, ensuring the protection of sensitive data and providing a reliable experience for merchants and customers.
