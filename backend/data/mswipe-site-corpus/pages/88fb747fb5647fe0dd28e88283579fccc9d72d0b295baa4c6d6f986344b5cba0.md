## What does Mswipe offer?

- Mswipe offers a host of offline and online payment solutions that can cater to your in-store or e-commerce requirements.

- Boombox: This is a payment soundbox that gives you sound and display alert on every QR payment. You have the option to choose from static QR or dynamic QR. On Dynamic QR, merchants feed in the payment amount and there is a unique QR code that flashes.

- POS machine: Card dip or NFC enabled contactless tap device

- Mswipe Payment Gateway – Accept online payments on your website or app via UPI, cards, and net banking.

- Pay by Link – Create and share payment links on WhatsApp, SMS, or email to collect payments remotely (no integration needed).

- Merchant App – Manage payments on the go: create links, view transactions, settlements, invoices, and get support.

- Merchant Portal – Web dashboard to track transactions, settlements, reports, and manage users/devices in one place.

- Mswipe Smart POS devices are designed for flexibility, accepting UPI payments through dynamic QR codes, contactless payments via NFC tap, and standard debit and credit card payments with the card dip option. This range of payment options ensures a seamless and convenient checkout experience for customers.
